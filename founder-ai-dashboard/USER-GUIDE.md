# User Guide: CEI Founder AI Science Dashboard

## What this dashboard does

The Founder AI Science Dashboard answers your scientific and business questions by searching real databases, analyzing evidence, and delivering sourced results you can use in decisions, decks, grants, and data rooms.

You ask a question in plain language. The system figures out which databases to search, what analysis to run, and how to present the answer. You never need to know which tool is doing the work.

## Getting started

When you first open the dashboard, you will be asked three quick questions:

1. **How do you work?** Scientist, founder, or both. This sets your default view.
2. **What are you building?** Diagnostics, medical devices, or therapeutics. This surfaces relevant tasks first.
3. **What would you like to do first?** Pick a starting task to jump straight in.

You can change any of these later in Settings.

## The two modes

Use the toggle in the top bar to switch between Science and Business mode.

- **Science mode** shows research, validation, and analysis tasks first. Outputs include full methodology details.
- **Business mode** shows regulatory, market, and competitive tasks first. Outputs lead with executive summaries.

Both modes have access to everything. Switching never loses your work.

## Asking a question

The search bar at the top of every screen is the fastest way to start. Type your question in your own words:

- "What is known about TP53 p.R175H as a therapeutic target?"
- "What FDA pathway fits a cfDNA liquid biopsy for CRC screening?"
- "Is there prior art for our antibody approach?"

The system will show you what it thinks you are asking and which sources it plans to query. If it gets the intent wrong, you can correct it.

## Understanding results

### Sources are attached to claims

Every important statement has a small colored chip next to it showing which source supports it. Click any chip to see the full source detail in the right panel, including the specific passage that supports the claim.

### Confidence is shown for every section

Look for the badge next to each section heading:

- **Strong evidence**: Multiple independent sources agree
- **Moderate evidence**: Some support exists, but coverage is incomplete
- **Limited evidence**: Very few sources address this directly
- **Sources disagree**: Different evidence leads to different conclusions (both are shown)
- **No data available**: The databases queried had nothing relevant

### When sources disagree

You will see a split view showing both positions with their supporting evidence. The system does not pick a winner for you. It shows what each side says and where the support comes from.

### What is missing

Gray blocks explicitly name what is unknown and what would resolve it. This is not vague hedging. It tells you what to do next.

## Review gates

When results touch areas like clinical decisions, regulatory strategy, patent opinions, or investment advice, you will see an orange banner. It names what kind of expert should review the output before you act on it.

The dashboard provides decision support. It does not provide legal, regulatory, clinical, or investment advice.

## Seeing what happened behind the scenes

Every result has a "What I did" section (collapsed by default). Expand it to see exactly which databases were queried, what parameters were used, and how long each step took. This is your audit trail.

## Reusing results

Results from one session are available as inputs to the next task. If you validated a target yesterday and want to build a patent screen today, the target evidence carries forward. You do not need to re-run searches.

## Exporting

Most output screens have an export section at the bottom. Choose your format (PDF, slides, Markdown, Word, CSV) and the system packages your results with sources attached.

Warnings will appear if evidence gaps remain unresolved or if citations are missing before export.

## Confidentiality

In Settings, you control the default confidentiality level for new sessions. You can require review before any external export. The dashboard will show a lock icon on any content marked confidential.

## Navigation

- **Home**: Your recent sessions, in-progress work, and alerts
- **New Task**: Browse all available task types or search for what you need
- **Research**: Your active conversational research session
- **History**: All past sessions
- **Library**: Saved deliverables and exported documents
- **Watchlist**: Competitors, targets, and topics you are monitoring
- **Settings**: Profile, provider connections, and data handling

## Tips

- Use follow-up suggestions at the bottom of any result to continue exploring without typing
- The system remembers your session. Come back tomorrow and pick up where you left off.
- If a result seems incomplete, check the provenance trail to see if a source timed out. The system will note this explicitly.
- Results labeled "Sample data for demonstration" are prototype mock data, not live results.
